# Mystic Mind author guide

Open [Mystic Mind Admin](https://mysticmind.co.bw/admin) and sign in with your own administrator email and password. The Overview shows saved content and unread enquiries. Use **View website** to open the public site in a separate tab.

## Write and publish an article

1. Open **Writings** and select **Add writing**, or choose an existing article.
2. Add a title, short introduction and publication date. The editor creates a web address from the title when you add a new article. Existing published addresses are locked. Optional search engine details are under the advanced controls.
3. Click in **Article text** and write or paste text from Word or Google Docs. Use **Paragraph style** for headings and the toolbar for bold, italic, underline, lists, quotes and links. **Clear formatting** removes styling from selected text. The word count and reading estimate update as you write.
4. Use the cover picker to choose an image or upload a JPG, PNG or WebP. Add alternative text describing the picture. Use **Insert image** to place a photo inside the writing. **Preview** shows how the article text will read.
5. Use **Save draft** to save your work in this browser. The dashboard also saves a browser draft automatically. Return using the same device and browser. Clearing its site data will remove browser drafts.
6. To keep a private draft in the CMS across devices, leave **Visible on website** off and choose **Publish changes**. To make the article public, turn **Visible on website** on and choose **Publish changes**. To take it off the site while keeping it in Writings, turn that setting off and publish the change.
7. If you need to delete an article, select **Remove**, confirm and publish the change. If the dashboard reports that the website changed while you were editing, copy the draft text you want to keep before choosing **Discard draft** and reopening the latest version.

**Publish changes** applies the pending changes in the workspace. Check their visibility before clicking it.

## Manage other content

- **Resources**. Add a resource, enter a title and description, and attach the PDF and its cover. Select existing files or upload new ones through the file picker. Uploads are limited to 3 MB per file. Turn on visibility and publish when ready.
- **Recommended books**. Add the title, author and your reason for recommending it. Choose a cover in the file picker, and use the up and down buttons to reorder the books.
- **Testimonials**. Enter the person's name, description and complete quotation, then select their approved photo.
- **Site and author**. Update your portrait and biography. **Mission** uses the same visual editor.
- **Enquiries**. Open a message and update its status after reading or replying. Use the dashboard to check for enquiries while email notifications are pending.
- **Media library**. Upload files once and select them again wherever you need them. Removing an attachment removes its reference from that item and keeps the original in the library.

## Password and sign-out

Open **Settings**, expand **Change password** and enter your current password followed by the new one twice. Use at least 12 characters. After saving, sign in again with the new password. Use **Sign out** when you finish, especially on a shared computer. If you forget your password, contact the site developer while email recovery is being prepared.
